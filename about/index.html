About ApexAI

ApexAI LLC builds navigation software for the phone people already carry. Its products tell people where things are and guide them there in real time, indoors and outdoors.

Whereabout turns an iPhone scan into a searchable map of a venue or a home, so that anyone can ask the way to a room, a booth, or an item and be guided there by voice. ApexVision guides pedestrians along the street, detecting walkable paths, hazards, and objects on the device. Neither requires beacons or equipment installed in the building.

ApexAI was founded in 2025 by Maged Shoman, PhD, and offers structured pilots to venues, event organizers, schools, healthcare providers, employers, and public agencies.
