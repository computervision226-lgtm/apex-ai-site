ApexVision

Three operating modes. All output is delivered through audio and haptics.

  • <200 msend-to-end latency, camera to audio cue
  • On-deviceall processing local; no data uploaded
  • Fully offlineoperates without Wi-Fi or cellular service
  • Patent pendingU.S. 19/760,891; Canada 3,319,882

Finder

Locates a requested object, such as keys, a doorway, or an available seat, and guides the user to within arm’s reach.

Safety

Identifies the walkable path, obstacles within it, and approaching hazards in real time.

Describe

Describes the surroundings on request, including layout, people, and signage, and answers follow-up questions.
