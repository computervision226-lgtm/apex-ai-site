Solutions

Two products on a shared spatial AI platform, together providing guidance from the street to a specific item indoors.

Whereabout

Searchable indoor maps that locate any destination or item and guide the user to it. Spaces serves venues and events; Rooms serves the home, down to the contents of individual drawers. Open testing on TestFlight is forthcoming.

Learn about Whereabout

ApexVision

Real-time pedestrian navigation, developed with blind and low-vision users. ApexVision detects walkable paths, hazards, and objects, and delivers spoken and haptic guidance at walking pace. All processing runs on the device, with no connectivity required. Currently in beta on iOS.

Learn about ApexVision

Whereabout

Indoor maps that guide people by voice to any destination or item. Designed first for blind and low-vision users and fully operable with VoiceOver.

Spaces

For venues and events. Mapped floor by floor with an iPhone, without survey crews or beacons. When a layout changes, only that area is rescanned.

Rooms

For the home. A one-minute scan maps each room and catalogs the contents of drawers and cabinets. Anyone the map is shared with can ask where an item is and be guided to it.

For kitchen and wardrobe manufacturers: a searchable storage map can ship with every installation.

Explore a sample room Visit whereabout.store

The app is free, including the first room. Scanning requires an iPhone Pro; locating items works on any iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

ApexVision

Three operating modes. All output is delivered through audio and haptics; no usable vision is required.

  • <200 msend-to-end latency, camera to audio cue
  • On-deviceall processing local; no data uploaded
  • Fully offlineoperates without Wi-Fi or cellular service
  • Patent pendingU.S. 19/760,891; Canada 3,319,882

Finder

Locates a requested object, such as keys, a doorway, or an available seat.

  • Voice-initiated object search
  • Directional audio guidance to within arm’s reach
  • Operates indoors and outdoors without GPS

Safety

Analyzes the pedestrian environment in real time, identifying the walkable path, obstacles within it, and approaching hazards.

  • Continuous walkable-path detection with obstacle alerts
  • Ground-level and overhead hazard warnings
  • Haptic and spatial-audio cues timed to walking pace

Describe

Provides a spoken description of the surroundings on request, including layout, people, and signage.

  • Reads visible signage and text
  • Answers spoken follow-up questions, such as “Where is the exit?”

Industry applications

A real-time, voice-first platform, applied wherever people need to find a destination or an item.

Vocational rehabilitation and veterans' services

Structured ApexVision pilots with staff training and outcome measures. Because no video or personal data leaves the device, privacy review is straightforward.

Transit and aviation

Spaces maps stations and terminals floor by floor, without beacons to install or maintain, and gives every traveler voice-guided directions.

Healthcare and senior care

Wayfinding for clinics and medical campuses, and Rooms for residents, with staff or family maintaining the map and residents locating items by voice.

Education

Districts and campuses license software rather than hardware, so students who are blind navigate buildings with the same tools as their peers.

Events and conferences

Venues are mapped before opening and updated as layouts change, so attendees receive accurate directions throughout the event.

Employers and workplaces

Workplace accommodations delivered as software on the employee’s own phone, at a fraction of the cost of dedicated assistive devices.
