Navigation agents by industry

Government and public services

Structured ApexVision pilots with staff training and outcome measures. Because the agent runs on the device and no video or personal data leaves the phone, privacy review is straightforward.

Transit and aviation

Spaces maps stations and terminals floor by floor, without beacons to install or maintain, and the agent gives every traveler voice-guided directions.

Healthcare and senior care

A wayfinding agent for clinics and medical campuses, and Rooms for residents, with staff or family maintaining the map and residents asking the agent where their things are.

Education

Districts and campuses license an agent rather than hardware, so every student and visitor finds their way around buildings with the same tools.

Events and conferences

Venues are mapped before opening and updated as layouts change, so the agent gives attendees accurate directions throughout the event.

Employers and workplaces

A wayfinding agent for offices and campuses, delivered as software on employees’ own phones rather than dedicated hardware.

For agencies and organizations

We conduct structured pilots of our navigation agents with venues and event organizers, transit operators, healthcare providers, school districts, public agencies, and employers. Each pilot includes staff training, defined outcome measures, and direct access to the founding team.
